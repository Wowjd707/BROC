assessment reports.

## Notes for whoever rebuilds this

- Cite the **table**, not just "EPA". Stationary and mobile combustion are
  different tables with different denominators.
- The Hub is reissued mid-year. Two files both called "2024" can differ, so
  record the edition and the retrieval date.
- Refrigerant GWPs here come from an IPCC assessment report. State which one:
  AR4, AR5 and AR6 give materially different values for the same refrigerant,
  and reporting frameworks differ on which they require.
