How it's built

Think of it as a house that's framed, plumbed and wired.

The hard parts are built and tested. The walls are up to you, so you can lay it out the way your company actually works.

Already built

  • The data model, with multiple sites and business units
  • The calculations for Scope 1, 2 and 3
  • Evidence and a change log on every reported number
  • Mapping to each reporting framework
  • Security that keeps each organization's data separate

Yours to set up

  • Your sites, fuels and waste streams
  • The emission factor sets you're licensed to use
  • Connections to your other systems
  • Your reporting calendar and review steps
  • Your company's name and logo

The factors were checked

The emission factors that come with it (EPA, eGRID, AIB, IPCC AR6) were checked line by line against the original publications. The calculations follow GHG Protocol, ESRS, GRI and SBTi rules.

It won't guess

If a factor is missing, BROC stops and tells you what to add. It won't fill in a number that just looks about right.

Safe to change with AI

The code comes with instructions for AI coding assistants, like "never make up an emission factor," so your team can customize it without breaking the math.

Is it a fit?

Who it's for

Probably a good fit if you

  • track Scope 1, 2 and 3 and have outgrown spreadsheets, but don't want a big enterprise contract
  • have CSRD, IFRS S2, CBAM, EUDR or SB 253 coming at you and want one set of data for all of it
  • get audited and need every number to trace back to a source
  • have someone who can run a web app, whether that's an analyst, your IT team or an AI coding assistant

Probably not a fit if you

  • want someone else to host and support it for you
  • need a ready-made Scope 3 factor library (you bring the ones you're licensed to use)
  • don't have anyone who can install and look after a web app
